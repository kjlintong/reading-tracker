## English (en-US)

**Short description** (≤80): Local-first reading tracker: shelves, stats & AI insights. No account.

**Full description:**
Readnest is a local-first reading tracker for people who keep a lot of books in their head — and want them on their phone instead.

Import your library from Goodreads, Open Library, Google Books, a CSV file, a photo of your shelf, or WeRead. Everything stays on your device in a private SQLite database; there is no account, no cloud, and no ads.

See your reading at a glance: category distribution, rating spread, and monthly progress. Get an AI-written reading report and personality tags based on what you actually read — not where you read it.

Plan daily reading and keep a streak. Capture notes and quotes. The app works fully offline and respects your privacy.

Now available in English, 简体中文, Deutsch, Français, and Español.

Readnest is open source (MIT).

**What's new (1.0.0):** First official release. Now in 5 languages (English, 简体中文, Deutsch, Français, Español). Local-first library, multi-source import, stats, AI reading report, reading plans with streaks, and notes — no account required.
